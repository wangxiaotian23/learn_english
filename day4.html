Day 4

会议中表达观点 Meetings

会议是英语口语的综合考场：要插得上话、表达得清楚、反对得不伤人、收尾收得漂亮。今天的重点是软化语气（softening）。

今日目标

  1. 掌握进入讨论的入场句型，敢于在会议上开口。
  2. 学会同意、部分同意、反对的三档表达，反对时对事不对人。
  3. 能礼貌打断、请求澄清，并在结束时做口头总结。

核心句型

① 进入讨论（拿到发言权）

Can I jump in here for a second?
我能插句话吗？
最自然的插话 · jump in
Sorry to interrupt, but I think there's an important angle we're missing.
抱歉打断一下，我觉得我们漏了一个重要的角度。
打断但有事要说
Building on what Sarah said, I think we could also...
接着 Sarah 说的，我觉得我们还可以……
接话 · building on，显得在听
I'd like to add one more thing before we move on.
在我们继续之前，我想再补充一点。
话题切换前争取发言
From the engineering side, I see a couple of risks worth flagging.
从工程角度，我看到了几个值得提出来的风险。
以角色身份发言 · flag a risk

② 表达观点：同意 / 反对（软化语气）

I totally agree with that approach.
我完全同意这个方案。
强烈同意
That's a fair point, but have we considered the maintenance cost?
有道理，但我们考虑过维护成本吗？
部分同意 + 抛出新问题
I see it a bit differently. My concern is mainly about the timeline.
我的看法略有不同。我主要担心的是时间安排。
委婉反对 · I see it a bit differently
I'm not sure that would work in practice, because our data volume is 10x bigger.
我不确定这在实际中可行，因为我们的数据量是它的十倍。
用事实反对 · not sure...because
Maybe we could try a middle ground — pilot it with one team first?
也许我们可以折中一下——先在一个团队试点？
提出折中 · middle ground
Just to play devil's advocate — what if the vendor raises prices next year?
让我唱个反调——如果供应商明年涨价怎么办？
play devil's advocate = 唱反调（对事不对人的免责声明）

③ 请澄清与推进会议

Sorry, I didn't quite catch that — could you say it again?
抱歉，我没太听清——能再说一遍吗？
没听清 · I didn't quite catch that
Could you elaborate on the migration plan a bit more?
关于迁移方案，你能再展开讲讲吗？
请展开 · elaborate
Let's take this offline — this is getting into the weeds.
这个我们线下再聊——现在有点陷入细节了。
把细节移出会议 · take it offline
To sum up: we'll go with option B, Sarah will own the migration, and we'll revisit the timeline on Friday.
总结一下：我们选方案 B，Sarah 负责迁移，周五再看时间安排。
会议收尾总结 · to sum up
Are we all aligned on this? Great, let's move on to the next item.
这点大家都一致吗？好，我们进入下一项。
对齐确认 · are we aligned

情景对话

场景一：技术选型讨论会（多轮观点交锋）

KevinTo kick things off: we need to pick a message broker for the new event pipeline. I've shortlisted Kafka and RabbitMQ. My vote goes to RabbitMQ — it's simpler to operate.先开个头：我们要为新的事件管道选一个消息中间件。我筛选了 Kafka 和 RabbitMQ。我投 RabbitMQ——运维更简单。
SarahI agree RabbitMQ is easier, but we're projecting 50,000 events per second at peak. That's Kafka territory.我同意 RabbitMQ 更简单，但我们预计峰值每秒五万条事件。那是 Kafka 的领域。
你Can I jump in here? I see it a bit differently. Our peak is high, but the average load is only around 2,000 per second. And the replay feature matters more to us than raw throughput — if we need to reprocess last week's events, Kafka makes that trivial.我能插句话吗？我的看法不太一样。峰值是高，但平均负载只有每秒两千左右。而且对我们来说，消息回放比裸吞吐量更重要——如果需要重新处理上周的事件，Kafka 做起来轻而易举。
KevinThat's a fair point about replay, but Kafka operations aren't trivial. Who's going to own the cluster?回放这点有道理，但 Kafka 的运维不简单。谁来负责这个集群？
你Good question. What if we use a managed service? Then the operational burden sits with the vendor, and we just handle the application side.问得好。如果用托管服务呢？运维负担就归供应商，我们只管应用层。
SarahJust to play devil's advocate — managed Kafka costs roughly four times more per month. Is the replay feature worth that budget?让我唱个反调——托管的 Kafka 每月成本大概是四倍。回放功能值这个预算吗？
你Honestly, maybe we could try a middle ground: start with managed RabbitMQ since it fits our current volume, and design the event schema so switching to Kafka later isn't painful. We'd revisit if we grow past the limits.说实话，也许可以折中：先用托管的 RabbitMQ，它符合我们当前的量；同时把事件 schema 设计成以后切换到 Kafka 也不痛苦。超过承载上限再重新评估。
KevinI can get behind that. Pragmatic and reversible.我可以接受。务实而且可逆。
你To sum up then: managed RabbitMQ for launch, schema designed for portability, and we'll revisit at 10,000 events per second sustained. Are we all aligned?那总结一下：上线用托管 RabbitMQ，schema 保证可迁移性，持续每秒一万条时再评估。大家都同意吗？
EveryoneAligned.一致。

场景二：跨部门会议，你没听清且被赶进度（应对技巧）

PM...so with the new rate-limiting in place, the integration should be seamless. Any concerns from engineering?……所以新的限流上线后，集成会很顺畅。工程这边有顾虑吗？
你Sorry, I didn't quite catch the part about rate limiting — could you elaborate? Which endpoints does it apply to?抱歉，限流那部分我没听太清——能展开讲讲吗？作用于哪些接口？
PMSure — it applies to the public API, 100 requests per minute per client.当然——作用于公开 API，每个客户端每分钟 100 次请求。
你OK, that's what I thought. One concern from the engineering side: our batch sync job currently makes around 300 requests per minute. We'd need to redesign it, which adds about a week of work.好，和我理解的一样。工程这边一个顾虑：我们的批量同步任务目前每分钟大约发 300 次请求。得重新设计，大概要多花一周。
PMHmm, I hadn't thought about the batch job. That's a good catch.嗯，我没想到批量任务。这个发现很重要。
你This is getting into the weeds, though — maybe we take it offline? I'll write up the options and share them by tomorrow, then we can decide async.不过这块越来越细了——要不线下聊？我把几个方案写出来明天发出来，我们异步决定就行。
PMYes, let's do that. Moving on to the next topic...好，就这么办。进入下一个话题……

场景三：你不同意上级的方案（向上管理的表达）

Tech LeadI think we should ship the feature on Friday as planned, even without full test coverage. The business pressure is real.我觉得周五按计划上线，即使测试覆盖不全。业务的压力是实打实的。
你I understand the pressure, and I do want us to hit the date. My concern is mainly about the payment path — if it breaks in production, the rollback and customer support cost would outweigh the week we'd gain.我理解压力，我也想按时完成。我主要担心的是支付链路——如果生产上出问题，回滚加客服成本会超过我们抢回来的这一周。
Tech LeadThat's a fair point, but can we scope it down somehow?有道理，但能不能缩小范围？
你Maybe a middle ground: we ship everything except the payment refactor, and that one piece goes out next Tuesday with full coverage. The business value ships Friday either way.也许可以折中：除了支付重构，其他都周五上线，支付那一块下周二带着完整测试发。无论如何，业务价值周五就能交付。
Tech LeadI can sell that to the business team. Good, let's go with that.这个说法我能跟业务团队交代。好，就这么定。

发音与连读提示

1. 插话前的 "Sorry to interrupt"：Sorry 读快些显得自然，重音落在 interrupt；整句不要太正式，语气放松反而更礼貌。

2. "I didn't quite catch that" 连读：didn't quite 连读时 /t/ 浊化，catch that 的两个 /tʃ/ 和 /ð/ 轻轻带过即可。

3. 反对时的语调策略："That's a fair point, but..." —— but 前面稍作停顿，but 后语调上扬再下降，既清晰又缓和。

练习任务

  • 跟读：场景一信息量最大，逐句跟读，模仿观点交锋时的节奏。
  • 三档表达练习：对「我们应该用微服务重构整个系统」这个观点，分别说出强烈同意、部分同意、委婉反对各一句话。
  • 总结练习：用 "To sum up: ..., ..., and ..." 句型总结你今天参加的任何一次真实会议，说 30 秒。
  • 打断练习：看英文技术演讲视频，在听到观点时暂停，练习用 "Can I jump in?" 接上自己的观点。
